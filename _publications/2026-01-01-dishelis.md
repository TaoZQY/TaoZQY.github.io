DisHelis studies resource allocation and model deployment for disaggregated large language model inference serving. The system considers heterogeneous GPU compute, network bandwidth, and stage-level heterogeneity in disaggregated inference pipelines.

The core idea is a hierarchical max-flow-graph-based resource allocation and model deployment algorithm, paired with a lightweight redeployment mechanism that adapts to fluctuating online conditions without costly restarts of ongoing services.
