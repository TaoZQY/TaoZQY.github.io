FAESR proposes a fine-grained bitrate adaptation method for neural-network-enhanced panoramic video streaming. The work integrates super resolution with energy awareness and addresses the often-neglected energy consumption of mobile devices in enhanced video streaming systems.

Key contributions include a real-measurement-based super-resolution power model, a joint QoE and energy-aware optimization formulation, and a branch-sequence deep reinforcement learning algorithm for tile-level bitrate allocation.
